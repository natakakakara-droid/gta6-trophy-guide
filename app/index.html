Road to the Platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

  1. Stage 1 — Story (Chapters 1–5). Play the story straight through. Earn the 13 story trophies, including the gold for the final mission.
  2. Stage 2 — Stranger questlines. Finish the Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone strands, plus 8 counted stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Shooting ranges (42 golds), drone races, races, stunts, challenges and the partner trophies.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles. WAiNK scans, card sets, vehicles, garages, meditation spots and exploring all of Leonida.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100%. After the story, the 100% Completion checklist unlocks (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). Finish it for the gold A Long Road, then the platinum pops.

Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate on the Chapter 1 balcony at Jason’s house (after Down to Earth, follow Lucia in the morning and Accept). There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving.
